# Website Redesign Brief: CCB Tech (ccbtec.com)

## 1. Project Overview & Brand Strategy
**Client/Brand:** Chartiers Creek Business Technology (CCB Tech)
**Core Offering:** Managed IT services, network infrastructure, hardware installation, and business website development.
**Target Audience:** Local small businesses and teams (specifically 5 to 50 employees) in the Pittsburgh area who are outgrowing DIY technology solutions and need reliable, ongoing support.
**Brand Tone:** Practical, plain-English, local, and trustworthy. The motto is "Less theater. More working equipment." The design must reflect this: clean, fast, and highly functional without unnecessary animations, heavy parallax effects, or corporate jargon.

## 2. Technical & Deployment Requirements
*   **Architecture & Performance:** The code should be lean, fast-loading, and mobile-first. 
*   **Templating/Framework:** Ensure compatibility with the current templating engine (e.g., EJS tags) and directory structures.
*   **Deployment Environment:** The final build must be optimized for deployment within an Azure environment. The architecture should be structured to cleanly support hosting multiple unique business websites with individual domains on this infrastructure.
*   **Form Integration:** The contact form must securely capture lead data and route it to the internal support/sales team.

## 3. Global UI/UX Guidelines
*   **Color & Typography:** Utilize the existing brand colors from the current CCB Tech logo. Typography should be highly legible (e.g., sans-serif modern fonts like Inter or Roboto).
*   **Navigation:** Sticky header with clear links: *Home, Services, How it Works, Contact*. 
*   **Action-Oriented:** The primary Call to Action (CTA) across the site is to get users to contact CCB Tech or start a project. Buttons should be high-contrast and clear.

## 4. Homepage Structure & Content Specifications

### A. Hero Section
*   **Visual:** Clean, professional background (subtle abstract tech or a high-quality, authentic image of a clean server rack/office setup).
*   **Headline:** Websites, networks, and hardware installation.
*   **Sub-headline:** Business technology support for small teams that need things to work.
*   **Primary CTA:** "Start a Project" (anchors to the contact form).
*   **Secondary CTA:** "See Who We Help" (anchors to The Hook).

### B. The "Hook" (Audience Identification)
*   **Layout:** Centered text block or split 50/50 with an icon/image.
*   **Purpose:** Speak directly to the 5–50 employee demographic.
*   **Copy:** "IT support built for growing teams. CCB Tech helps local businesses look professional online, connect their office cleanly, and get everyday equipment installed without turning the project into a maze."

### C. Expanded Services Grid
*   **Layout:** A 3-column grid (or 3 clean horizontal cards) detailing the core offerings. 
*   **Card 1: Business Websites**
    *   *Copy:* Lean, credible sites that explain the business, work on mobile, and make the next step obvious. Hosted securely and built to convert.
*   **Card 2: Network & Cloud Infrastructure**
    *   *Copy:* Secure, reliable connections for your office and the cloud. From configuring physical Wi-Fi, switches, and routers, to administering SaaS applications and managing secure Azure cloud environments, we ensure your data is accessible and running efficiently.
*   **Card 3: Help Desk & Hardware Support**
    *   *Copy:* A local IT partner for the daily technology hurdles. We provide plain-English help desk support, on-site troubleshooting for the Pittsburgh area, and clean installations for new workstations, access points, and printers.

### D. The "Why CCB Tech?" Section
*   **Layout:** Two-column layout emphasizing the benefit of vendor consolidation.
*   **Purpose:** Emphasize that CCB Tech is a single local partner capable of handling everything from physical hardware to web presence and cloud administration, eliminating the need to manage multiple disconnected IT vendors.

### E. How it Works (Process)
*   **Layout:** A simple 1-2-3 horizontal stepper or vertical timeline.
*   **Heading:** Less theater. More working equipment.
*   **Step 1:** Listen to the goal and inspect what is already in place.
*   **Step 2:** Recommend the simplest reliable path before you spend money.
*   **Step 3:** Build, connect, test, and explain the finished setup clearly.

### F. Trust Signals (Social Proof)
*   **Layout:** A subtle horizontal banner or logo carousel.
*   **Content:** Placeholders for client logos or vendor partner logos (e.g., Microsoft, Azure, etc.) to establish credibility.

### G. Footer & Lead Capture
*   **Layout:** A clean, accessible contact form built directly into the lower section of the homepage (Name, Email, Phone, Company Size, "Tell us what needs built, connected, or installed").
*   **Footer Links:** Standard copyright, privacy policy, and quick navigation links. Prominently display "Pittsburgh-area support" and contact details.
